# OwnerOS AI — Qualification Scorecard

Score each prospect during or after the discovery call.
**Total: 20 points. Recommended threshold to propose: 12+**

---

## Scoring Rubric

### 1. Business Fit (0–4 points)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 4 | Owner-led service business, 5–30 employees, primary verticals (med spa, contractor, dental, roofing, law, real estate, training) |
| 3 | Owner-led service business, 3–50 employees, secondary verticals |
| 2 | Owner-led but product or e-commerce; or larger than 50 employees |
| 1 | Marginal fit — not owner-led or not a service business |
| 0 | No fit |

**Score: ___/4**

---

### 2. Pain Clarity (0–4 points)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 4 | Owner can name 3+ specific operational pains with concrete examples (leads lost, follow-up failed, everything is in my head) |
| 3 | Owner acknowledges 2 clear pains with some specificity |
| 2 | Owner has vague awareness of problems but can't articulate clearly |
| 1 | Thinks everything is mostly fine but wants to "learn about AI" |
| 0 | No apparent pain; shopping without intention |

**Score: ___/4**

---

### 3. Budget Fit (0–4 points)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 4 | Clearly can afford $1,500 Audit with no friction; discussion of implementation budget ($5K–$15K) is comfortable |
| 3 | No objection to $1,500 Audit; uncertain about larger investment |
| 2 | Hesitation at $1,500; needs convincing |
| 1 | Mentioned budget concerns; looking for cheap or free options |
| 0 | Stated they have no budget for this kind of work |

**Score: ___/4**

---

### 4. Decision Authority (0–4 points)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 4 | Owner is on the call and can make the decision directly |
| 3 | Owner is on the call but mentions needing to discuss with partner |
| 2 | Not the owner; office manager or ops person without authority |
| 1 | Unclear who decides; multiple stakeholders without a clear process |
| 0 | No decision maker accessible |

**Score: ___/4**

---

### 5. Urgency and Readiness (0–4 points)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 4 | Clear urgency: "we need to fix this now," recent incident, growth moment, hiring issue |
| 3 | Wants to move forward in the next 30 days |
| 2 | Interested but no clear timeline; "sometime this quarter" |
| 1 | Thinking about it for later; "maybe next year" |
| 0 | No urgency; exploring with no intention to move |

**Score: ___/4**

---

## Total Score: ___/20

| Score | Recommendation |
|-------|---------------|
| 16–20 | Strong fit — move to Audit proposal immediately |
| 12–15 | Good fit — propose Audit with clear next step |
| 8–11 | Marginal — send follow-up materials, revisit in 30–60 days |
| 0–7 | Not a fit at this time — thank and archive |
