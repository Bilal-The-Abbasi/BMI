# OwnerOS AI — Role Definitions and Responsibilities

*Updated as the business grows. Version 1.0 — Two-Founder Model.*

---

## Founder 1 — Marketing, Sales, and Client Communication

**Primary responsibilities:**
- Brand positioning and messaging
- Website copy and updates
- Landing pages
- Lead generation strategy and execution
- Cold outreach (email + LinkedIn)
- Discovery calls (primary lead)
- Proposals and pricing conversations
- Sales follow-up
- Client communication (day-to-day emails, weekly updates)
- AI workflow design (strategy and content)
- Prompt library management
- Reporting insights and analysis
- Content creation (LinkedIn, blog, lead magnets)
- Case study writing
- Business development (partnerships, referrals)

**Shared responsibilities with Founder 2:**
- Strategy decisions
- Pricing decisions
- Proposal review
- Demo delivery
- Training delivery
- Client success decisions
- Quarterly business review

---

## Founder 2 — Project Management and Delivery Operations

**Primary responsibilities:**
- Project management for all client engagements
- Workflow mapping and documentation
- Implementation planning and timelines
- Tool configuration and automation build
- Task assignment and project board management
- Rollout planning and sequencing
- Quality assurance review
- Delivery operations and process adherence
- Handoff document creation
- Notion Command Center setup
- Technical troubleshooting (automations, integrations)
- Vendor and tool management

**Shared responsibilities with Founder 1:**
- Strategy decisions
- Pricing decisions
- Proposal review (input on feasibility)
- Demo delivery
- Training delivery
- Client success decisions
- Quarterly business review

---

## Decision-Making Protocol

| Decision Type | Who Decides |
|--------------|------------|
| Client proposal (scope + price) | Both founders agree |
| Client communication (routine) | Founder 1 |
| Project timeline change | Founder 2 (notify Founder 1) |
| Scope change (minor) | Founder 2 (change request process) |
| Scope change (significant) | Both founders |
| New service offering | Both founders |
| Hiring decision | Both founders |
| Pricing changes | Both founders |
| Tool/vendor purchase < $100/month | Founder 2 |
| Tool/vendor purchase > $100/month | Both founders |

---

## Weekly Meeting Cadence

**Founder Check-In (Every Monday, 30 minutes):**
- Pipeline review
- Active project status
- Blockers
- This week's priorities
- Anything that needs a joint decision

**Client Review (Every Friday, 15 minutes):**
- All active clients: on track, behind, or at risk?
- Any client issues that need attention before the weekend?
- Weekly updates sent?

---

## Communication Guidelines

- Use [internal tool — Slack/email/Notion] for all project communication
- If it's important, it's in the project tool — not just a verbal conversation
- All client-facing decisions are logged
- Never make significant commitments to a client without the other founder knowing
