### 1. Purpose

This policy describes how artificial intelligence (AI) tools are used in the delivery of OwnerOS AI services, what human oversight is in place, and what responsibilities the client and OwnerOS AI each carry regarding AI-generated content and outputs.

---

### 2. How AI Is Used in Our Engagements

OwnerOS AI uses AI tools to support — not replace — human judgment. Specific uses include:

| AI Application | Description |
|----------------|-------------|
| Draft generation | Creating first drafts of emails, SOPs, follow-up messages, and summaries |
| Data summarization | Summarizing call notes, intake forms, or customer communications |
| Prompt assistance | Generating prompt templates for client teams to use |
| Workflow suggestions | Recommending automation sequences based on business context |
| Reporting insights | Generating weekly summary language from dashboard data |
| FAQ and script drafting | Drafting FAQ answers and customer communication scripts |

---

### 3. Human Review Requirement

**All AI-generated content is reviewed by a human before it is delivered to the client or used in customer-facing communications.**

This includes:
- Email templates and response drafts
- Customer-facing messages and sequences
- SOP content
- Report language
- FAQ and script content

The client is responsible for reviewing and approving all AI-generated content before it is used in customer-facing or employee-facing contexts. OwnerOS AI provides reviewed drafts — final approval rests with the client.

---

### 4. What AI Will Not Be Used For (Without Explicit Human Approval)

AI tools will not generate — and OwnerOS AI will not deliver — content that:

- Makes specific revenue, ROI, or business performance guarantees
- Provides medical advice, medical recommendations, or clinical guidance
- Provides legal advice or legal conclusions
- Provides financial advice, tax recommendations, or investment guidance
- Makes pricing commitments not approved by the client
- Makes representations about regulated claims (insurance, healthcare, legal outcomes)
- Impersonates a specific person without their knowledge
- Is intended to deceive customers about the nature of communication

---

### 5. AI Disclosure Considerations

Depending on the client's industry, jurisdiction, and use case, there may be legal or ethical requirements to disclose when AI is used in customer communications. Examples include:

- Healthcare providers (HIPAA, patient communication standards)
- Legal firms (bar association guidelines on AI use)
- Financial services (FINRA, SEC, state regulations)
- Consumer-facing businesses (FTC guidance on AI disclosure, state consumer protection laws)

**OwnerOS AI does not provide legal or compliance guidance on AI disclosure requirements.** The client is responsible for determining and complying with all applicable disclosure requirements in their industry and jurisdiction, in consultation with qualified legal counsel.

---

### 6. Data Handling

AI tools used in this engagement may process business data including:
- Lead and contact information
- Customer communication samples
- Internal process descriptions
- Staff notes and meeting summaries

The client agrees to:
- Not share protected health information (PHI), financial account data, social security numbers, or other regulated personal data through AI tools unless an appropriate data processing agreement is in place
- Review what data is being shared with which tools, as described in the project's Data Access Checklist
- Inform OwnerOS AI of any industry-specific data restrictions before the project begins

---

### 7. No Guarantee of AI Output Quality

AI outputs are provided as starting points, not final deliverables. OwnerOS AI makes no guarantee that AI-generated content will be:
- Factually accurate in all cases
- Free from bias or errors
- Compliant with industry regulations
- Appropriate for all use cases without modification

The client is responsible for reviewing all AI-assisted content before use.

---

### 8. Client Responsibility

The client agrees to:
- Assign a designated reviewer for all AI-generated content before it is used externally
- Not deploy AI-generated customer communications without human review
- Inform OwnerOS AI immediately if any AI-generated content appears inaccurate, harmful, or inappropriate
- Ensure internal use of the AI prompt library follows the guidelines in this policy

---

### 9. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated as AI tools evolve and as regulatory guidance develops. OwnerOS AI will notify clients of material changes to this policy with at least 14 days' notice.
