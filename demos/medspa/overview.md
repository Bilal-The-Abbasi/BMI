# Demo: Med Spa Lead Follow-Up System

**Business:** Glow Med Spa (fictional demo business)
**Location:** [City], [State]
**Size:** 12 employees
**Services:** Botox, fillers, laser treatments, facials, body contouring
**Revenue:** ~$1.8M/year

---

## The Before Scenario

**What was happening before OwnerOS AI:**

Glow Med Spa was receiving 30–40 new inquiries per week from:
- Website contact form
- Instagram DMs
- Missed phone calls
- Referrals (verbal)

**The problem:**
- Website form sent an email to a shared inbox that 3 people monitored
- Instagram DMs were checked once or twice a day (sometimes)
- Missed calls had no callback system — if the caller didn't leave a voicemail, they were forgotten
- When a response did go out, it took 4–6 hours on average
- No follow-up sequence existed — one response, then silence

**The result:**
- Estimated 8–12 missed leads per week that never received a response
- Leads who did receive a response often didn't hear back after the first touch
- The owner was manually following up on "VIP" leads that no one else had handled
- No tracking — zero visibility into which channels produced leads

---

## The After Scenario

**What OwnerOS AI built:**

### The Front Door
1. All inquiries now flow into one GoHighLevel CRM pipeline
2. Every new lead gets an automated SMS within 90 seconds:
   > "Hi [Name]! This is Sarah from Glow Med Spa 💆 We received your message and will be in touch within the hour. In the meantime, you can browse our services here: [link]"
3. Missed calls trigger an automatic text-back within 2 minutes
4. All lead sources are tagged and tracked

### The Follow-Up Desk
5-step follow-up sequence for every new lead who doesn't book within 24 hours:

| Step | Timing | Channel | Message |
|------|--------|---------|---------|
| 1 | 90 seconds | SMS | Instant acknowledgment + link |
| 2 | 2 hours | SMS | "Hi [Name], I wanted to reach out personally…" |
| 3 | Day 2 | Email | Specific service interest + booking link |
| 4 | Day 5 | SMS | "Still thinking about it? We have [X] spots left this month" |
| 5 | Day 10 | Email | Value add: "What to expect at your first consultation" |

---

## Lead Flow Map

```
New Lead Entry Points:
  Website form → Auto-SMS (90 sec) → CRM pipeline "New Lead"
  Instagram DM → Manual check → CRM pipeline "Social Lead"
  Missed call → Auto text-back (2 min) → CRM pipeline "Phone Lead"
  Referral → Staff intake form → CRM pipeline "Referral Lead"

All leads → Follow-Up Sequence (Day 1 → Day 2 → Day 5 → Day 10)

Lead converts → Appointment booked → Reminder sequence → Post-visit review request

Lead goes cold → 30-day inactive → Reactivation campaign
```
