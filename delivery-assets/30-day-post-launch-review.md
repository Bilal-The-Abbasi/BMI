# OwnerOS AI — 30-Day Post-Launch Review Template

Run this call 30 days after project handoff. Duration: 30–45 minutes.

---

## Pre-Call Prep

- Review the client's Weekly Summary logs from the past 4 weeks
- Note any automations that may have had issues
- Review the original bottleneck report — have any been resolved?
- Note any quick wins from the Audit that haven't been addressed yet
- Prepare retainer proposal if appropriate

---

## Call Agenda

### 1. Check-In (5 minutes)

> "It's been about 30 days since we handed off. How has it been going overall? What's your general sense of how things are running?"

---

### 2. Room-by-Room Review (15 minutes)

Go through each room that was built:

**Room 1: Front Door**
> "How has lead intake been working? Any leads slipping through that we should catch?"
> "Is the automated response working as expected?"

**Room 2: Follow-Up Desk**
> "Are the follow-up sequences running? Any issues with messages going out incorrectly?"
> "Has your team noticed a difference in lead follow-up consistency?"

**Room 3: Playbook**
> "Is the team using the SOPs? Any that need updating based on how things actually work?"
> "Any FAQs that need to be added or changed?"

**Room 4: Workroom**
> "Is the task board being used? Any resistance from the team?"
> "Any recurring tasks that need to be added or adjusted?"

**Room 5: Control Room**
> "Is the weekly summary being completed? Is it useful for you?"
> "Is the dashboard showing you what you need to see?"

---

### 3. What's Working (5 minutes)

> "What's one thing that's working better than it was 30 days ago?"

Note this for case study use.

---

### 4. What Needs Adjustment (5 minutes)

> "Is there anything in the system that's not working quite right or needs to be changed?"

Log each issue and schedule fixes (either included or as a change order).

---

### 5. New Opportunities (5 minutes)

> "Now that these systems are running, have you noticed any new gaps or areas where you'd want to improve?"

This is the natural opening for a retainer or next project conversation.

---

### 6. Next Steps (5 minutes)

Based on the call, options:
- Everything is working — close the project and open the retainer conversation
- Minor adjustments needed — schedule 1–2 hour fix session (often included in scope)
- New room or feature needed — propose a follow-on engagement or retainer

---

## Post-Call Actions

- [ ] Send call summary email within 24 hours
- [ ] Log any system issues and assign to fix queue
- [ ] Update project status in CRM
- [ ] Send retainer proposal if appropriate
- [ ] Send testimonial request if project went well (see testimonial-request.md)
- [ ] Note case study opportunity if applicable
