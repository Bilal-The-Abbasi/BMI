## What Is OwnerOS AI?

Most small businesses don't need more AI tools. They need a cleaner way to run their business.

OwnerOS AI installs a practical, AI-supported operating system that organizes leads, follow-up, SOPs, tasks, dashboards, and owner visibility into one coherent system — so the business runs with less chaos, less manual effort, and less dependence on the owner's memory.

## The 5 Rooms Framework

Every client engagement is organized around the 5 Rooms of an owner-led business:

1. **The Front Door** — Lead intake, routing, source tracking, initial response
2. **The Follow-Up Desk** — Nurture, scheduling, quoting, reminders, reactivation
3. **The Playbook** — SOPs, scripts, FAQs, brand voice, training docs
4. **The Workroom** — Tasks, projects, approvals, recurring checklists, handoffs
5. **The Control Room** — Owner dashboard, weekly summary, metrics, AI insights

---

## The OwnerOS Method (5 Phases)

1. **Map** — Document how the business actually runs today
2. **Score** — OwnerOS Readiness Score (0–100 across 10 dimensions)
3. **Build** — Implement missing systems room by room
4. **Train** — Onboard the team to use the new systems
5. **Optimize** — Monthly review, iteration, and expansion
