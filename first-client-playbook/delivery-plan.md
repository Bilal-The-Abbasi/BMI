# OwnerOS AI — First Client Delivery Plan

**Default client:** Med Spa (adapt for other industries)
**Service:** OwnerOS Growth System ($12,500)
**Timeline:** 8 weeks

This playbook guides the delivery of the first (and subsequent) client engagements.
It is designed for a two-person team operating without additional staff.

---

## Pre-Sales (Before Contract)

**Founder 1 (Marketing):**
- [ ] Conduct discovery call using the Discovery Call Script
- [ ] Score prospect with the Qualification Scorecard
- [ ] Present the Audit first ($1,500) — don't jump to Growth System until Audit is complete
- [ ] Send follow-up email same day with summary

**Founder 2 (PM):**
- [ ] Review discovery call notes
- [ ] Provide input on feasibility and timeline before proposal is sent

**Both:**
- [ ] Agree on the proposal before it goes out
- [ ] Present proposal on a call — never just email and wait

## Week 0: Contract + Kickoff Prep (Days 1–3)

| Task | Owner | Day |
|------|-------|-----|
| Contract signed and filed | Founder 2 | Day 1 |
| Invoice sent (50% upfront) | Founder 1 | Day 1 |
| Project created in PM tool | Founder 2 | Day 1 |
| Notion Command Center created (client workspace) | Founder 2 | Day 2 |
| Welcome email sent with intake form + policies | Founder 1 | Day 1 |
| Access Request Checklist sent | Founder 2 | Day 1 |
| Risk Register opened | Founder 2 | Day 2 |
| Internal kickoff call (both founders, 30 min) | Both | Day 2 |
| Kickoff call with client scheduled | Founder 1 | Day 2 |

**Key risk:** Client doesn't complete intake or provide access.
**Mitigation:** Intake form has a deadline (3 business days). Follow up proactively on Day 2.

## Week 2: Score + Build Plan (Days 11–14)

| Task | Owner | Day |
|------|-------|-----|
| Score finalized across all 10 dimensions | Founder 2 | Day 11 |
| Build plan documented (what gets built, in what order, in what tools) | Founder 2 | Day 11–12 |
| Build plan reviewed by Founder 1 | Founder 1 | Day 12 |
| Build plan presented to client (30 min) | Both | Day 13 |
| Client approves build plan | Client | Day 14 |
| Week 2 update sent | Founder 1 | Day 14 |

**Gate:** Do not begin building until client approves the build plan in writing (email reply counts).

---

## Week 3: Build — Front Door + Follow-Up Desk (Days 15–21)

**Founder 2 builds (with Founder 1 reviewing copy/messaging):**
- [ ] CRM pipeline set up (or configured in existing tool)
- [ ] Lead intake form created and connected to CRM
- [ ] Missed call automation live (tested)
- [ ] Initial response template written and approved
- [ ] 5-step follow-up sequence built (messages written by Founder 1, automation built by Founder 2)
- [ ] Source tracking configured

**Review gate:** Client reviews all customer-facing message templates before the sequence goes live.
Human Review Checklist completed.

**Week 3 update sent:** Founder 1

---

## Week 4: Build — Playbook (Days 22–28)

**Founder 2 builds structure. Founder 1 writes content:**
- [ ] Notion Playbook page set up in Command Center
- [ ] 5 core SOPs documented (based on highest-impact gaps from audit)
- [ ] FAQ Knowledge Base created (20 entries, sourced from discovery call)
- [ ] Brand voice guide created (tone, approved language, escalation rules)
- [ ] Client reviews and approves all Playbook content

**Founder 1 writes all customer-facing content, Founder 2 builds in Notion.**

---

## Week 5: Build — Workroom + Control Room (Days 29–35)

**Founder 2 builds:**
- [ ] ClickUp task board set up with correct team members
- [ ] Recurring weekly checklists configured
- [ ] Team responsibility document created
- [ ] Owner weekly dashboard created (Looker Studio or Notion)
- [ ] Weekly summary template set up

**Client action required:** Provide team member names and roles for task board setup.

---

## Week 6: QA + Training Prep (Days 36–42)

- [ ] Full QA review of all 5 Rooms (Founder 2 leads, Founder 1 reviews content)
- [ ] Human Review Checklist completed for all AI-generated content
- [ ] All automations tested end-to-end with real test data
- [ ] Training materials prepared (Loom videos planned — Founder 1 outlines, records)
- [ ] Quick-reference guides written for front desk and staff
- [ ] Handoff document drafted

---

## Week 7: Training (Days 43–49)

| Session | Attendees | Duration | Led by |
|---------|-----------|----------|--------|
| Team training | Front desk + staff | 2 hours | Founder 2 (structure) + Founder 1 (content) |
| Owner walkthrough | Business owner | 1 hour | Both |
| Loom library shared | All staff | Self-paced | Recorded by Founder 1 |

**After training:**
- [ ] Confirm team can demonstrate using the systems without prompting
- [ ] Any gaps addressed before handoff

---

## Week 8: Handoff + Launch (Days 50–56)

- [ ] Final handoff document completed and delivered
- [ ] All access verified by client
- [ ] Final invoice sent (remaining 50%)
- [ ] Launch sign-off from client
- [ ] 30-day check-in call scheduled
- [ ] Case study conversation initiated (informal: "How has the first week felt?")
- [ ] Testimonial request sent at Day 30

## Success Metrics

By project completion, the client should have:

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Average lead response time | < 5 minutes (automated) |
| Follow-up sequences active | ≥ 1 for all new leads |
| SOPs in Playbook | ≥ 5 |
| Team trained and using the system | 100% of relevant staff |
| Owner dashboard reviewed | Weekly (Monday) |
| Owner's OwnerOS Readiness Score improvement | +20–35 points from baseline |

## Risks and Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| Client unavailable for feedback/approvals | 48-hour approval window in SOW; pause timeline if exceeded |
| Scope creep ("Can you also...") | Change request process; refer to SOW |
| Tool access not provided | Access checklist sent Day 1; follow up proactively |
| Founder 2 is the bottleneck for build | Parallelize where possible; Founder 1 handles all copy in advance |
| Client team doesn't adopt the system | Training is required, not optional; schedule multiple sessions |
