## Client Responsibility Policy
**OwnerOS AI — Standard Engagement Terms**

*Version 1.0*

---

### Purpose

This document outlines the responsibilities that the client (the business receiving OwnerOS AI services) agrees to take on during and after an OwnerOS AI engagement. Clear client responsibilities protect both parties and ensure the systems we build together actually work.

---

### 1. Access and Participation

The client agrees to:

- Designate one primary point of contact who is empowered to make decisions about the engagement
- Provide timely access to tools, accounts, and information as requested (see Data Access Checklist)
- Participate in scheduled discovery calls, review sessions, and training sessions
- Respond to requests for information, feedback, and approvals within agreed-upon timeframes (default: 48 business hours)
- Ensure relevant staff members are available for training sessions

**Impact of delays:** Project timelines are contingent on client participation. Delays caused by unavailability of the client team may extend the project timeline at no fault of OwnerOS AI.

---

### 2. Content Review and Approval

The client is responsible for:

- Reviewing all AI-generated content, templates, scripts, and SOPs before approving them for use
- Ensuring that all customer-facing communications meet the client's legal and compliance standards
- Obtaining legal, medical, or regulatory review of any content that may require professional oversight (e.g., healthcare communications, legal disclaimers, financial statements)
- Providing feedback on drafts within the agreed review window

**OwnerOS AI does not guarantee that any template, script, or workflow is compliant with all applicable laws and regulations in the client's industry. The client is responsible for compliance.**

---

### 3. Tool Access and Credentials

The client agrees to:

- Share necessary tool credentials and access through secure methods only (no credentials via email or unsecured chat)
- Maintain password security for all accounts used in the engagement
- Remove OwnerOS AI access to all accounts upon project completion or termination (see Access Request Checklist)
- Notify OwnerOS AI immediately if any shared credentials are suspected to be compromised
- Not share OwnerOS AI-provided credentials, prompts, or proprietary templates with third parties without written consent

---

### 4. Data Privacy

The client agrees to:

- Not share protected personal data (HIPAA-regulated PHI, financial account numbers, SSNs, etc.) through AI tools unless a Data Processing Agreement is in place
- Inform OwnerOS AI of any data privacy restrictions that apply to their industry before the project begins
- Ensure their own privacy policy reflects any new data collection or processing activities resulting from the engagement
- Comply with all applicable data privacy regulations (GDPR, CCPA, HIPAA, etc.) as they apply to the client's business

---

### 5. Legal and Regulatory Compliance

The client is solely responsible for:

- Ensuring all systems, workflows, and communications built in the engagement comply with applicable laws
- Obtaining any required licenses, permits, or professional approvals for their business operations
- Not using OwnerOS AI deliverables for any purpose that is illegal, deceptive, or in violation of applicable regulations
- Ensuring that AI-assisted customer communications comply with the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA), CAN-SPAM, CASL, or other relevant communication regulations

---

### 6. No Outcome Guarantees

The client acknowledges:

- OwnerOS AI delivers operational systems, templates, and workflows. We do not guarantee specific business outcomes, revenue increases, or lead conversion improvements.
- Results depend on client adoption, team engagement, business market conditions, and factors outside OwnerOS AI's control.
- Any projections or estimates discussed during the sales process are illustrative, not guaranteed.

---

### 7. System Ownership and Maintenance

After project handoff:

- The client owns all systems, SOPs, templates, and workflows delivered as part of the engagement
- The client is responsible for maintaining, updating, and training staff on the systems unless an active retainer is in place
- OwnerOS AI is not responsible for system degradation, automation failures, or process drift that occurs after the engagement ends without ongoing support

---

### 8. Confidentiality

The client agrees to:

- Keep OwnerOS AI's proprietary frameworks, templates, and methodologies confidential
- Not reproduce, resell, or distribute OwnerOS AI deliverables without written consent
- Not share OwnerOS AI proposal documents, pricing, or internal delivery materials with competitors
