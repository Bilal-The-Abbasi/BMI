# OwnerOS AI — Testimonial Request Templates

Send these at the 30-day post-launch check-in, or any time the client has expressed strong positive feedback.

---

## Email 1: Testimonial Request (After 30-Day Check-in)

**Subject:** One quick ask — [Business Name]

Hi [Name],

Glad to hear things are running well.

I have one ask: would you be willing to share a short testimonial about working with us?

It can be 2–4 sentences in your own words — no polish needed. What was the situation before, what we helped with, and how things feel now.

You can reply directly to this email or leave a Google review here: [link]

If it's easier to jump on a 10-minute call and I'll turn your words into a draft you can approve, I'm happy to do that too.

We're building out our case study library and your voice would mean a lot.

Thank you,
[Your Name]

## Testimonial Question Prompts

If the client needs help knowing what to say, send these prompts:

1. What was the main operational problem you had before working with OwnerOS AI?
2. What did we build or fix during the engagement?
3. What has changed for you since — in the business or for you personally?
4. Would you recommend us to another business owner? Why?
