### Content Quality

- [ ] All copy is accurate for this specific client (no generic placeholders remaining)
- [ ] Business name, contact info, and details are correct throughout
- [ ] No lorem ipsum or template text left in deliverables
- [ ] Tone matches the client's brand voice guide
- [ ] All links in the deliverable work correctly

### AI-Generated Content

- [ ] Human Review Checklist completed for all AI-generated content
- [ ] No false claims, guaranteed outcomes, or unverified statements
- [ ] No medical, legal, or financial advice included
- [ ] All AI-generated content reviewed and approved by a human
- [ ] AI-generated content reviewed against the client's specific context (not generic)

### Automations and Technical

- [ ] All automations tested end-to-end with real test data (not just manually triggered)
- [ ] Automations trigger correctly from all intended input sources
- [ ] Failure handling is in place (error notifications, fallback logic)
- [ ] All automated messages reviewed for accuracy before go-live
- [ ] CRM fields are correctly mapped
- [ ] Source tracking is working

### SOPs

- [ ] All SOPs are accurate to how this business actually works (not a generic template)
- [ ] Steps are clear enough for a new employee to follow
- [ ] Escalation rules are defined for every SOP
- [ ] SOPs are accessible to the team (correct Notion permissions)
- [ ] Owner has reviewed and approved all SOPs

### Dashboards

- [ ] Dashboard data is accurate (tested with real data, not sample data)
- [ ] All metrics are defined (owner knows what each number means)
- [ ] Update frequency is documented
- [ ] Owner knows how to access and read the dashboard

### Training Readiness

- [ ] All training materials are complete before the training session
- [ ] Loom videos are recorded and accessible
- [ ] Quick-reference guides are printed or accessible on mobile
- [ ] Training agenda is clear and shared with attendees in advance

### Handoff Readiness

- [ ] Handoff document is complete with all links and access details
- [ ] All systems are in the client's own tools (not just in OwnerOS AI's account)
- [ ] Client team can access everything without our help
- [ ] 30-day check-in is scheduled

## Common QA Failures (Watch For These)

1. **Generic content** — A deliverable that could have been for any business in the industry, not this specific one
2. **Untested automations** — Built but never triggered with real data
3. **Locked-out client** — Systems still require OwnerOS AI credentials to access
4. **Unapproved AI content** — AI draft sent without human review
5. **Missing SOPs** — Built automations without the corresponding SOP for when the automation fails
6. **Broken links** — Dashboard or Notion links tested on one browser/device but broken on mobile
7. **Wrong permissions** — Client staff have too much or too little access to Notion/ClickUp
