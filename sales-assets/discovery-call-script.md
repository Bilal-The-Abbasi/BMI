# OwnerOS AI — Discovery Call Script

**Call duration:** 30–45 minutes
**Goal:** Understand the business, qualify fit, build trust, move toward Audit proposal
**Who books this call:** Inbound leads, outreach responses, referrals

---

## Before the Call

- Review intake form or LinkedIn profile
- Note what industry they're in and approximate size
- Identify the most likely pain points for that industry
- Have the Qualification Scorecard open

---

## Opening (2 minutes)

> "Hi [Name], thanks for taking the time — I'm [Your Name] from OwnerOS AI. Before I say much about what we do, I'd love to hear about you and your business first. Is that okay?
>
> Can you give me a quick overview — what does [Business Name] do, how long have you been operating, and how big is your team right now?"

*Let them talk. Don't interrupt. Take notes.*

---

## Business Understanding (10 minutes)

After they describe the business, ask:

**Operations:**
> "Walk me through a typical week — what's on your plate that you wish wasn't?"

> "When a new lead comes in, what actually happens? Take me through it step by step."

**Pain identification:**
> "What's the thing that keeps falling through the cracks, no matter how many times you try to fix it?"

> "If I could wave a wand and remove one bottleneck from your business right now — what would it be?"

**Owner dependency:**
> "How often do things stop or slow down because they needed to come through you?"

> "Could you take a week off right now without the business struggling?"

**Tools:**
> "What tools are you using — CRM, project management, communication, scheduling?"

> "Are they connected, or are you copying and pasting between them?"

---

## Current State Assessment (8 minutes)

> "Let me ask you a few quick questions about specific areas. These map to how we diagnose operational health:"

| Area | Question |
|------|----------|
| Lead capture | "When a lead comes in from multiple channels, is there one place they all land?" |
| Follow-up | "How is follow-up being managed right now — is there a system or does someone do it manually?" |
| SOPs | "If a new person started tomorrow, where would they go to learn how things get done?" |
| Tasks | "How does your team know what to work on each day? Is there a task board or is it communicated verbally?" |
| Reporting | "Where do you go right now to see how the business is performing — leads, revenue, follow-up, tasks?" |

---

## Introduce OwnerOS AI (5 minutes)

> "Based on what you've shared, this sounds like a really good fit for what we do.
>
> We work with owner-led businesses — exactly your size and profile — to install what we call an operating system for the business. We organize everything around five areas: where leads come in, how follow-up happens, where your processes live, how your team manages work, and what you see as the owner every week.
>
> We do this in phases: first we map and score how the business runs today, then we build what's missing, train the team, and stay involved to keep improving.
>
> The best way to get started is with our OwnerOS Audit — it's $1,500, takes about 2 weeks, and gives you a complete picture of your business operations plus a clear roadmap. Most clients move forward from there, but there's no obligation."

---

## Handle Questions / Objections

*[Use the Objection Handling Guide for specific objections]*

Common ones to prepare for:
- "We already have [tool]" → "The tool isn't the issue — it's the process around it."
- "What's the ROI?" → "The audit will quantify that for your specific situation."
- "We're too busy right now" → "That's usually a sign you need this most."

---

## Next Steps (3 minutes)

If it's a strong fit:
> "I'd like to propose we move forward with the OwnerOS Audit. I'll send you a short intake form to complete before we start. From there we'll schedule the 60-minute discovery session and have your report to you within 2 weeks.
>
> Does $1,500 work, and does that timeline fit your calendar?"

If they need a proposal first:
> "Let me send you a one-page overview and we can reconnect on [specific day]. I'll include the Audit details and what we'd recommend based on what you've shared."

If it's not a fit:
> "Based on what you've described, I don't think we're the right fit right now. [Reason]. I appreciate you taking the time and I hope it was still useful. If things change, don't hesitate to reach back out."

---

## After the Call

**Within 2 hours:**
- Log notes in CRM
- Score using Qualification Scorecard
- Send follow-up email (see follow-up templates)
- If moving forward: send intake form link and invoice

**Within 24 hours:**
- Confirm next step is in calendar
- If proposal requested: send it
