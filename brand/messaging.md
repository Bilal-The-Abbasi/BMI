## FAQ Page Copy

**Q: Is this just automation software?**
A: No. We build the strategy, process design, templates, and training that make automation actually work. The software is just one part of the operating system.

**Q: Do we need a big tech stack to get started?**
A: No. We work with what you have. If you need new tools, we recommend the simplest options that fit your business — not the most complex.

**Q: Will AI replace my staff?**
A: No. AI in our system handles routine tasks and reduces manual work — but your team still runs the business. We never promise AI replaces humans. We promise it makes humans faster.

**Q: How long does it take?**
A: An OwnerOS Audit takes about 2 weeks. A QuickStart implementation takes 3–4 weeks. A full Growth System takes 6–8 weeks. Timeline depends on how quickly the client team can engage.

**Q: What's included in the Audit?**
A: A 60-minute discovery call, review of your current workflows and tools, OwnerOS Readiness Score (0–100), visual workflow map, bottleneck report, quick wins list, and a 90-day roadmap. Delivered as a branded 12–15 page PDF report.

**Q: Can we start small?**
A: Yes. The $1,500 OwnerOS Audit is designed as a low-risk entry point. Most clients move forward into a QuickStart or Growth System after seeing the audit results — but there's no obligation.

**Q: Do you work with businesses outside these industries?**
A: Our primary focus is owner-led service businesses with 3–50 employees. If your business fits that profile, we're likely a good fit regardless of industry. Reach out and we'll tell you quickly.

**Q: What happens after the project?**
A: We offer an OwnerOS Partner Retainer — monthly optimization, new SOPs and automations, dashboard reviews, and quarterly strategy sessions. Many clients continue with us after their initial project.
