## Risk Scoring Key

| Likelihood | Score | Impact | Score |
|-----------|-------|--------|-------|
| Low (unlikely) | 1 | Low (minor inconvenience) | 1 |
| Medium (possible) | 2 | Medium (delays or rework) | 2 |
| High (likely) | 3 | High (project failure or client harm) | 3 |

**Risk Score = Likelihood × Impact** (1–9)
- 1–3: Monitor
- 4–6: Active mitigation required
- 7–9: Escalate immediately
