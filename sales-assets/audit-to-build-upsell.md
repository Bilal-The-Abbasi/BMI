# OwnerOS AI — Audit-to-Build Upsell Script

Use this during or after the Audit presentation call to transition into a proposal for implementation.

---

## When to Use This Script

- You've just finished presenting the Audit report
- The client has heard their Readiness Score and understands their gaps
- There's positive energy and engagement
- You want to move them naturally toward the next investment

---

## The Setup

At the end of the Audit presentation, after walking through findings:

> "Before I open it up for questions — let me show you the roadmap we put together based on everything we found."

*[Show the 90-day roadmap slide or page from the report]*

> "This roadmap represents what we'd recommend doing over the next 90 days if you wanted to move from where you are now — [score, e.g., 28/100] — to where a well-run business in your industry typically lands [target score, e.g., 65–75/100].
>
> Some of this you could do yourself. The quick wins section especially — those are things you can start on this week without us.
>
> But if you wanted to move through this systematically and fast, this is what a full engagement with us would look like."

---

## The Transition

> "I want to show you two options based on your situation."

**Option A — QuickStart (if one gap is dominant):**
> "If I had to pick the single highest-impact thing to fix right now, it's [Room/area]. That's where you're losing the most — and it's the most fixable in a short timeframe.
>
> Our QuickStart is $5,000 and focuses entirely on that one area. We'd have it built, trained, and running in 3–4 weeks.
>
> That's the option I'd recommend if you want to move fast and see results quickly."

**Option B — Growth System (if multiple gaps are significant):**
> "Looking at your score, you have gaps in [3+ areas]. The honest answer is they're connected — if we fix just one, the others will still create friction.
>
> Our Growth System at [price] addresses all five rooms over 6–8 weeks. It's the most complete path to getting your business running the way you want.
>
> This is what I'd recommend if you want to solve it fully and not piece it together."

---

## Handle the Hesitation

**If they say "I need to think about it":**
> "Totally fair. Can I ask — is there a specific part you're unsure about? Sometimes it's the investment, sometimes it's the timing, sometimes it's whether we'd actually be a good fit. I'd rather understand what's on your mind."

**If they mention price:**
> "I hear that. Let me ask — if price wasn't a factor, is this the right direction for your business?
>
> [If yes]: Okay, let me show you what a scoped-down version would look like. We could start with just [one Room] for $5,000 and expand later.
>
> [If no]: Then let me understand what's not sitting right, because I'd rather get that out now than send you a proposal you won't act on."

**If they want to start small:**
> "The QuickStart is designed exactly for that — one room, proven result, lower commitment. Most clients who start there come back for more within 90 days because they see it work.
>
> The audit cost is credited toward the QuickStart, so you're effectively paying $3,500 net if we move forward now."

---

## The Close

> "Based on what we found and what you've told me, here's what I'd recommend:
>
> [State recommendation clearly — one sentence]
>
> I can have a Statement of Work to you by [day — within 48 hours]. If you can sign by [date], we can start [week].
>
> Does that timeline work for you?"

*Pause. Let them respond. Do not fill the silence.*
