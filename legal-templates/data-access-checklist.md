## Section 2: Credential Security Rules

**All credentials must follow these rules:**

- [ ] Never share credentials via standard email
- [ ] Use a secure method: 1Password shared vault, LastPass shared folder, or OwnerOS AI's secure intake link
- [ ] Create project-specific logins where possible (rather than sharing the owner's personal login)
- [ ] Document which accounts have 2FA enabled — provide backup codes if needed for project tools
- [ ] Do not share master admin accounts if a limited-access role is available

## Section 4: AI Tool Data Sharing Rules

Based on the data categories above, record what data may and may not be shared with AI tools:

**Approved for AI processing:**
- [ ] Customer first names and inquiry types (no full contact info)
- [ ] Internal process descriptions and SOP content
- [ ] Anonymized communication examples
- [ ] Non-personal business data (revenue ranges, lead counts, etc.)

**Not approved for AI processing (without legal review):**
- [ ] Full PHI (name + health condition + contact)
- [ ] Financial account numbers or payment data
- [ ] Social security numbers or government IDs
- [ ] Full attorney-client correspondence
- [ ] Employee performance or HR data
