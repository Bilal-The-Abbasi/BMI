# OwnerOS AI — Internal Sales Pipeline

---

## CRM Pipeline Stages

### Stage 1: Lead — Contacted
- New lead has been identified and initial outreach sent
- No discovery call yet

**Actions:** Send initial email/message. Log in CRM with source.
**Move to next stage when:** They respond and agree to a call.

---

### Stage 2: Lead — Discovery Call Scheduled
- Call is on the calendar
- Intake form sent (if applicable)

**Actions:** Review their business/LinkedIn before call. Prepare questions.
**Move to next stage when:** Discovery call is completed.

---

### Stage 3: Lead — Discovery Call Completed
- Call has happened
- Qualification scorecard completed

**Actions:** Score the lead. Send follow-up email. Decide: propose Audit, propose full service, or nurture.
**Move to next stage when:** Proposal or Audit offer is sent.

---

### Stage 4: Lead — Proposal Sent
- Proposal or Audit offer is in their inbox
- Waiting for decision

**Actions:** Follow up at Day 3, Day 7. Address objections. Book a presentation call if needed.
**Move to next stage when:** They accept (→ Stage 5) OR they decline (→ Stage 10 or 11).

---

### Stage 5: Client — Audit In Progress
- Audit has started
- Intake form, discovery call, report building

**Actions:** Follow Audit Checklist. Deliver on time. Present findings.
**Move to next stage when:** Audit delivered and presented.

---

### Stage 6: Client — Implementation In Progress
- Contract signed, project underway
- Active project (QuickStart, Growth System, or Full Buildout)

**Actions:** Follow Delivery Plan. Send weekly updates. Complete QA.
**Move to next stage when:** Build phase complete, entering training.

---

### Stage 7: Client — Training
- Build is complete
- Training sessions scheduled/in progress

**Actions:** Deliver team training + owner walkthrough. Confirm adoption.
**Move to next stage when:** Training completed, handoff confirmed.

---

### Stage 8: Client — Handoff

**Actions:** Deliver handoff document. Collect final payment. Schedule 30-day check-in.
**Move to next stage when:** Project closed, client using the system.

---

### Stage 9: Client — Retainer Active
- Ongoing monthly engagement

**Actions:** Deliver monthly report. Build new items. Conduct check-in call.
**Stay here:** As long as retainer is active.

---

### Stage 10: Closed — Won
- Engagement completed successfully (no retainer)
- Good candidate for testimonial and case study

---

### Stage 11: Closed — Not Won
- Prospect didn't move forward

**Actions:** Log reason. Add to nurture sequence. Check back in 90 days.

---

### Stage 12: Nurture — Long-Term
- Not ready now but a real prospect

**Actions:** Send value email monthly. Note any relevant trigger events (new business, growth, etc.).

---

## Weekly Pipeline Review (Every Monday, 15 minutes)

| Question | What to check |
|----------|--------------|
| Any leads stuck in Stage 2–3 for 7+ days? | Follow up or re-qualify |
| Any proposals at 7+ days with no response? | Send Day 7 follow-up email |
| Any active projects behind schedule? | Escalate and adjust |
| Any retainer clients due for monthly call? | Confirm calendar |
| New leads this week? | Log source + qualification score |
