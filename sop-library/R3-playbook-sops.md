# Room 3: The Playbook — Process and Communication SOPs

---

## SOP R3-01: Customer Complaint — Initial Response

**Owner:** Front desk / Manager
**Trigger:** Customer expresses dissatisfaction via phone, email, text, or in person
**Tools:** Phone, Email, CRM

### Steps
1. Listen fully without interrupting. Do not defend or explain until the customer has finished
2. Acknowledge the experience with genuine empathy:
   > "I'm really sorry to hear that — that's not the experience we want you to have."
3. Do NOT offer refunds, discounts, or service credits without manager approval
4. Do NOT admit fault on behalf of the business in a way that could create liability — say "I understand" not "That was our mistake"
5. Log the complaint in CRM immediately: date, nature of complaint, customer name, what was said
6. Notify the manager or owner within 30 minutes (same day if after hours)
7. If the complaint is severe or the customer is threatening legal action — escalate to owner immediately and stop the conversation

### AI Assist Opportunity
Use the "Complaint Acknowledgment Draft Prompt" for a compassionate, non-committal draft response.

### Quality Checklist
- [ ] Customer allowed to fully express concern without interruption
- [ ] Empathy expressed without admitting liability
- [ ] Manager notified within 30 minutes
- [ ] CRM logged with complaint details

### Escalation Rule
Escalate to owner immediately if: customer mentions attorney, threatens a public review, mentions a health/safety issue, or is asking for a significant refund.

## SOP R3-02: Negative Review Response

**Owner:** Owner or designated manager
**Trigger:** Negative review posted on Google, Yelp, or other platform
**Tools:** Google Business Profile / Review platform, Email, CRM

### Steps
1. Monitor reviews at least once per week (set up Google Alerts or use review management tool)
2. When a negative review is found, do not respond immediately — allow 24 hours to process and draft thoughtfully
3. Use the Negative Review Response Draft Prompt (AI-assisted draft, human-approved final)
4. Response structure:
   - Acknowledge the experience
   - Express regret without admitting fault
   - Invite offline resolution: "We'd love to make this right — please contact us at [email/phone]"
   - Keep it brief (under 100 words)
5. Owner approves final response before posting
6. After posting response, reach out privately via phone/email to attempt resolution
7. Log in CRM: review platform, date, response sent, resolution outcome

### AI Assist Opportunity
"Negative Review Response Prompt" — generates a professional, empathetic response for review and approval.

### Quality Checklist
- [ ] Response drafted and reviewed before posting
- [ ] Response is professional, calm, empathetic
- [ ] No defensive language or personal attacks
- [ ] Offline resolution offered
- [ ] Owner approved before posting

### Escalation Rule
If review mentions a legal matter, false facts, or the reviewer appears to be a competitor — consult an attorney before responding.

## SOP R3-03: New Client Onboarding

**Owner:** Front desk / Account manager
**Trigger:** New client signs contract or makes first purchase
**Tools:** Email, Onboarding form, CRM, Notion

### Steps
1. Send welcome email within 24 hours (use Welcome Email Template)
2. Share the Client Onboarding Checklist with the client
3. Create client record in CRM with status: "Active Client"
4. Send access or intake documents as relevant to service
5. Schedule a kickoff call or onboarding session within 3 business days
6. Add client to weekly update cadence (if applicable)
7. Log in CRM: onboarding started, kickoff date, primary contact

### Quality Checklist
- [ ] Welcome email sent within 24 hours
- [ ] CRM record updated to "Active Client"
- [ ] Kickoff scheduled
- [ ] All intake documents sent

### Escalation Rule
If client's first experience involves a complaint, scheduling issue, or access problem — escalate to manager immediately. First impressions matter.

## SOP R3-04: New Employee Onboarding

**Owner:** Manager / Owner
**Trigger:** New team member's first day
**Tools:** Notion (Playbook), CRM, Email, Scheduling tool, Task board

### Steps
1. Create their accounts in all relevant tools before their first day
2. Day 1: 1-hour orientation covering: business mission, the 5 Rooms, their specific role, tools they'll use
3. Share the Command Center / Playbook access with them
4. Assign a "Playbook reading" task: review all SOPs in their role's Room within first 3 days
5. Walk them through the top 5 SOPs for their role in their first week
6. Assign a "buddy" — another team member they can ask questions of in Week 1
7. Week 2: check-in meeting — what's still unclear? Update SOPs based on their questions
8. Add to all relevant task boards, recurring checklists, and communication channels

### Quality Checklist
- [ ] All tool access set up before Day 1
- [ ] Day 1 orientation completed
- [ ] Playbook access given
- [ ] Top 5 SOPs reviewed in Week 1
- [ ] Week 2 check-in scheduled

### Escalation Rule
If new employee raises concerns about safety, conduct, or a compliance issue — escalate to owner immediately.

## SOP R3-05: Weekly Team Meeting

**Owner:** Manager / Team Lead
**Trigger:** Every [day, e.g., Monday at 9 AM]
**Tools:** Video/in-person, Task board, Weekly Summary report

### Steps

**Before the meeting (5 min prep):**
- Pull up the Weekly Summary from last week
- Pull up the task board — what's overdue?
- Note any issues from the past week

**Meeting agenda (30 minutes max):**
1. Last week's wins (5 min) — what went well?
2. Last week's issues (5 min) — what fell short? What's the fix?
3. This week's priorities (10 min) — each person's top 3 tasks
4. Blockers (5 min) — what needs a decision or resource?
5. Owner updates (5 min) — anything from leadership

**After the meeting:**
- Log summary in Command Center meeting notes
- Ensure tasks from meeting are created and assigned in the task board

### Quality Checklist
- [ ] Meeting started on time
- [ ] Agenda followed (no overrun)
- [ ] Action items captured and assigned
- [ ] Meeting notes logged

### Escalation Rule
If a significant issue is raised (staffing, client complaint, safety, compliance) — schedule a separate follow-up. Don't try to solve it in the weekly meeting.

## SOP R3-06: Customer FAQ Update Process

**Owner:** Operations manager / Designated staff
**Trigger:** Staff ask a question that isn't in the FAQ; customer asks something new; an answer changes
**Tools:** Notion (FAQ Knowledge Base)

### Steps
1. When staff or a customer asks a question not in the FAQ — log it immediately in the "FAQ Queue" (a simple running note or task)
2. Weekly: review the FAQ Queue with the owner or manager
3. For each queued item:
   - Draft the approved answer (can use AI to draft, human must approve)
   - Add to Notion FAQ Knowledge Base with correct category and status
   - Notify team: "New FAQ added: [topic]"
4. When an existing answer changes (pricing, policy, service change), update the FAQ immediately
5. Quarterly: review all FAQs for accuracy

### Quality Checklist
- [ ] FAQ Queue reviewed weekly
- [ ] New entries added within 5 business days of being identified
- [ ] Owner approves all customer-facing answers before publishing
- [ ] Team notified of updates

### Escalation Rule
If an FAQ answer involves pricing, legal compliance, health/safety, or policy — owner must approve before the answer is marked Active.
