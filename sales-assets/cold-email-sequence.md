## Personalization Rules

1. Always reference the specific business name
2. Reference a specific pain that's common in their industry
3. Never make revenue guarantees in cold outreach
4. Never claim to know their problems — always frame as a pattern you've seen
5. Keep emails under 150 words
6. One clear CTA per email (usually: book a call or reply)
7. No "Hope this email finds you well" openers
