# OwnerOS AI — Case Study Strategy

---

## Goal

Get 3 strong case studies in the first 6 months. Use these to:
- Build website social proof
- Support sales conversations ("Here's what we did for a business like yours")
- Create LinkedIn content
- Anchor proposals

---

## Case Study 1: Beta/Known Contact — Discounted Engagement

**Target:** A business owner you already know and trust
**Discount:** 25–30% off standard pricing
**Goal:** Prove the delivery model, get documented results, build a reusable case study

**Offer language:**
> "We're taking on a small number of beta clients as we launch OwnerOS AI. In exchange for a reduced rate and your honest feedback, we'll build your operating system with the same rigor we'll deliver to every client — and document the results together."

**What to measure:**
- Baseline metrics (Day 1): OwnerOS Readiness Score, response time, follow-up rate, no-show rate, lead sources tracked
- Post-project metrics (Day 30): same metrics, compare
- Owner sentiment: qualitative feedback before and after

**What to document during the project:**
- Screenshots of the before state (inbox, lack of automation, no dashboard)
- Screenshots of the after state (CRM pipeline, automation active, dashboard)
- Weekly summary comparison: Week 1 vs. Week 8
- Owner quote in their own words
- Video testimonial if willing

---

## Case Study 2: Local Service Business

**Target:** A med spa, contractor, dental office, or roofing company in your area
**Discount:** 15–20% off if they agree to full case study and testimonial
**Goal:** Local proof point; referral source; relatable to your core target market

**Approach:**
- Attend a local business networking event (chamber, BNI, etc.)
- Introduce yourself: "We help local [industry] businesses install AI-supported operating systems"
- Offer: "We're looking for 1–2 local businesses to partner with at a discounted rate in exchange for documenting the results"

**What to measure:**
- Lead response time (before/after)
- Follow-up rate (before/after)
- Owner time savings (self-reported)
- OwnerOS Score improvement

---

## Case Study 3: Education/Training or Professional Service

**Target:** Private training school, coaching company, HR firm, CPA, or law firm
**Discount:** 15% if they allow an anonymized case study
**Goal:** Diversify vertical proof; appeal to a slightly different buyer

## Case Study Format

**Title:** How [Business Type] in [City] Went from [Problem] to [Result]

**Sections:**
1. **The Business:** Industry, size, what they do
2. **The Problem:** What they were struggling with (in their words + our diagnosis)
3. **The OwnerOS Readiness Score:** Where they started
4. **What We Built:** Summary of systems implemented (use 5 Rooms language)
5. **The Results:** Specific, measurable outcomes where possible; qualitative where not
6. **Owner Quote:** Direct quote from the owner
7. **The Score After:** New OwnerOS Readiness Score

**Metrics to include (if permission given):**
- Response time improvement
- Follow-up rate change
- No-show rate change
- Lead volume visibility (can quantify without dollar claims)
- Time owner saves per week (self-reported)

**What to avoid:**
- Revenue guarantees or ROI promises
- Specific revenue figures (unless owner explicitly approves)
- Any medical or clinical claims (for healthcare clients)

---

## Permission Language

Include in the project agreement or as a separate addendum:

> "We would love to document this engagement as a case study to share on our website and in sales materials. The case study would describe the challenges you faced, the systems we built, and the results you experienced. We will share the draft with you for approval before publishing. You can choose to be identified by name or remain anonymous. You can withdraw permission at any time before publication."

```

[2 paragraphs: business background + what brought them to OwnerOS AI]

Notable gaps: [Top 3 dimensions]

## What We Built
[3–5 bullet points: key systems implemented]

## The Results (30 Days After Launch)
- [Metric 1]
- [Metric 2]
- [Metric 3]
- Owner quote: "[In their own words]"

## OwnerOS Score (After)
Total: [X/100] — improvement of [X points]
```

## Testimonial Request Process

1. At the 30-day check-in: ask for verbal feedback first ("How has the first month felt?")
2. If positive: send the Testimonial Request email (Template 1 in delivery-assets)
3. If they prefer: offer to draft it from a 10-minute call and send for approval
4. Collect: written testimonial + photo (optional) + permission to use name and business type
5. Store in: `case-studies/testimonials/`
