# Room 4: The Workroom — Task and Operations SOPs

---

## SOP R4-01: End-of-Day Task Review

**Owner:** All staff (individual) + Manager (team review)
**Trigger:** Every business day, 30 minutes before close
**Tools:** Task board (ClickUp/Notion)

### Steps
1. Open the task board and filter to "My Tasks"
2. Mark all completed tasks as Done
3. Move any incomplete tasks: update the due date if needed, add a note explaining the delay
4. Flag any blockers — tasks that can't be completed without something from someone else
5. Review tomorrow's tasks — are you prepared?
6. Manager reviews the team task board:
   - Any overdue tasks with no update?
   - Any blockers that need resolution tonight or tomorrow morning?
7. Log in meeting notes if any major issue arose

### Quality Checklist
- [ ] All tasks reviewed and updated
- [ ] No completed tasks left as "In Progress"
- [ ] Blockers flagged and assigned to resolver
- [ ] Tomorrow's priorities confirmed

### Escalation Rule
If a task that affects a client deliverable or customer-facing process is delayed — notify the relevant person immediately, don't wait until tomorrow morning.

## SOP R4-02: Invoice Follow-Up — Overdue Payment

**Owner:** Office manager / Bookkeeper / Owner
**Trigger:** Invoice is 5+ business days past due date with no payment received
**Tools:** Accounting software (QuickBooks/FreshBooks/Stripe), Email, Phone, CRM

### Steps
1. Day 5 past due: send friendly reminder email using the Invoice Reminder Template #1:
   > "Hi [Name], just a quick note — Invoice #[X] for [amount] was due on [date]. Please let us know if you have any questions or if there's been an issue with payment."
2. Day 10 past due: send follow-up #2 — more direct, same professional tone
3. Day 15 past due: phone call from manager or owner — discuss the situation
4. Day 20 past due: send final notice with specific consequences (as defined in contract)
5. Log every communication in CRM
6. Do not continue delivering services to an account with invoices 20+ days overdue (owner decision required)

### AI Assist Opportunity
"Invoice Follow-Up Draft Prompt" — generates a professional, firm reminder message.

### Quality Checklist
- [ ] Day 5 reminder sent
- [ ] Day 10 follow-up sent
- [ ] Day 15 call attempted
- [ ] All communications logged in CRM

### Escalation Rule
Any invoice over $[threshold] that is 15+ days overdue requires owner notification. Do not send a final notice or make collection decisions without owner approval.

## SOP R4-03: Change Request Handling

**Owner:** PM (Founder 2) + Client owner
**Trigger:** Client requests a change to the scope of work during an active project
**Tools:** Email, SOW document, CRM, Project board

### Steps
1. Receive and acknowledge the change request — do not agree, disagree, or estimate in the initial conversation
   > "Thank you — let me review the scope and get back to you within 1 business day with what this would look like."
2. Review the current SOW — is this in scope or out of scope?
3. If out of scope: prepare a change order with:
   - Description of the change
   - Additional time required
   - Additional investment (if any)
   - Impact on current timeline
4. Share change order with client for review and signature before any additional work begins
5. Log in project notes: "Change request received — [date] — [description] — [status: pending/approved/declined]"
6. Update project timeline if change order is approved

### Quality Checklist
- [ ] Change acknowledged within 1 business day
- [ ] SOW reviewed before committing to scope
- [ ] Change order prepared for out-of-scope requests
- [ ] Work not started until change order signed
- [ ] Timeline updated

### Escalation Rule
If a change request would significantly increase the project scope, timeline, or budget — both founders discuss before presenting to client.

## SOP R4-04: Monthly Reporting

**Owner:** Operations manager / PM
**Trigger:** Last business day of the month
**Tools:** Dashboard, Google Sheets, CRM, Task board

### Steps
1. Pull the monthly summary data: leads received, lead sources, appointments booked, conversion rate, revenue (if tracked), tasks completed vs. overdue
2. Note top 3 wins this month and top 3 issues
3. Compile into the Monthly Executive Summary template (see dashboards folder)
4. AI Summary Prompt: provide the raw numbers and ask AI to draft a brief narrative summary
5. Review AI draft — adjust for accuracy before sharing
6. Send to owner via email or post in Command Center by [first business day of next month]

### Quality Checklist
- [ ] Data pulled from all relevant sources
- [ ] Report drafted and reviewed before sending
- [ ] AI narrative reviewed for accuracy
- [ ] Report sent/posted within 1 business day of month end

### Escalation Rule
If any metric shows a significant decline (e.g., lead volume down 30%+), flag with a note and discuss with owner before sending the report.

## SOP R4-05: Content Approval Process

**Owner:** Marketing lead (Founder 1) + Owner
**Trigger:** New content created for website, social media, email, or ads is ready for review
**Tools:** Notion, Email, Google Docs

### Steps
1. Content creator completes draft and places it in the "Content Review" folder with status "Needs Review"
2. Tag the owner or approver with a deadline for review (default: 2 business days)
3. Reviewer checks:
   - [ ] Accurate and factual
   - [ ] On-brand tone (see Brand Voice Guide)
   - [ ] No unverified claims, guaranteed outcomes, or pricing errors
   - [ ] Compliant with relevant rules (e.g., healthcare promotion rules if applicable)
4. Reviewer approves (status → "Approved") or requests changes (status → "Needs Edits" with specific notes)
5. Upon approval: schedule or publish content
6. Log: content published, date, channel, link

### Quality Checklist
- [ ] Content tagged for review with clear deadline
- [ ] All review criteria checked
- [ ] No content published without approval
- [ ] Published content logged

### Escalation Rule
Any content that makes performance claims, includes testimonials, or targets a regulated health/legal/financial topic requires owner review before publication.
