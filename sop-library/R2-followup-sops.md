# Room 2: The Follow-Up Desk — Follow-Up SOPs

---

## SOP R2-01: Quote / Estimate Request

**Owner:** Sales staff / Front desk
**Trigger:** Customer requests a price quote or estimate for services
**Tools:** CRM, Email, Quote template

### Steps
1. Capture all details needed for an accurate quote: service type, scope, timeline, and any special requirements
2. If additional information is needed, ask in the same conversation — don't send a partial quote
3. Prepare the quote using the approved Quote Template (do not create custom pricing without manager approval)
4. Send the quote via email within 2 business hours (same day is the goal)
5. Log in CRM: "Quote sent — [date] — [amount/range] — [service]"
6. Create a follow-up task: "Quote follow-up call/text — [2 business days after send]"

### AI Assist Opportunity
Use the "Quote Cover Email Prompt" to draft a professional, personalized email to accompany the quote.

### Quality Checklist
- [ ] All necessary information collected before quote prepared
- [ ] Quote sent within 2 hours
- [ ] CRM logged with quote amount and status
- [ ] Follow-up task created

### Escalation Rule
Any quote over $[threshold set by owner] requires owner approval before sending. Unusual scope or scope not covered by standard pricing — escalate to owner.

## SOP R2-02: Quote Follow-Up (3-Day No Response)

**Owner:** Front desk / Sales staff
**Trigger:** 3 business days have passed since a quote was sent with no response
**Tools:** CRM, Email, SMS

### Steps
1. CRM task triggers: "Quote follow-up — Day 3"
2. Send a brief, non-pushy follow-up message using the Quote Follow-Up Template:
   > "Hi [Name], just following up on the quote I sent on [date] for [service]. Do you have any questions, or is there anything I can clarify?"
3. Log in CRM: "Quote follow-up sent — Day 3"
4. Create next follow-up task: "Quote final check-in — Day 7"

### AI Assist Opportunity
Use the "Quote Follow-Up Draft Prompt" to generate a warm, specific follow-up message.

### Quality Checklist
- [ ] Follow-up sent within 1 day of task trigger
- [ ] Message is personalized to the specific service/quote
- [ ] CRM updated
- [ ] Day 7 follow-up task created

### Escalation Rule
If the quote is for a large project and still unanswered at Day 7, escalate to owner for a personal call.

## SOP R2-03: Appointment Scheduling

**Owner:** Front desk / Receptionist
**Trigger:** Customer requests to book an appointment, consultation, or service
**Tools:** Scheduling software (Calendly, Acuity, or booking system), CRM, Email/SMS

### Steps
1. Confirm the service type being requested and the customer's preferred time
2. Check availability in the scheduling system
3. Offer 2–3 time options if they haven't already selected one
4. Book the appointment and confirm all required details: name, contact info, service, duration, any prep instructions
5. Send confirmation email/text using the Appointment Confirmation Template (automated or manual)
6. Schedule reminder automation: 24-hour reminder + 1-hour reminder (automated if possible)
7. Create CRM record or update existing record with appointment details

### AI Assist Opportunity
If a customer asks questions about what to expect, use the "Pre-Appointment FAQ Response Prompt."

### Quality Checklist
- [ ] Appointment confirmed in scheduling system
- [ ] Confirmation sent to customer
- [ ] Reminders scheduled or automated
- [ ] CRM record updated

### Escalation Rule
If customer has a special accommodation request or mentions a health concern that affects the service — escalate to the provider or manager before confirming.

## SOP R2-04: Appointment Reminder (24 Hours Before)

**Owner:** Front desk (or automation)
**Trigger:** Appointment is 24 hours away
**Tools:** SMS platform, Email, Scheduling software

### Steps
1. Automated system sends 24-hour reminder (if automation is in place — skip manual steps)
2. If manual: send reminder message using the 24-Hour Reminder Template
3. Include: customer name, service, date, time, location (or video link), and any prep instructions
4. If no confirmation received, send a follow-up text 2 hours before the appointment

### Quality Checklist
- [ ] Reminder sent/confirmed 24 hours before appointment
- [ ] All key details included (service, time, location, prep)
- [ ] Follow-up sent if no confirmation

### Escalation Rule
If customer replies to cancel or reschedule — see SOP R2-05. If customer mentions a health or safety concern — escalate to the provider immediately.

## SOP R2-05: No-Show Follow-Up

**Owner:** Front desk / Receptionist
**Trigger:** Customer does not appear for scheduled appointment within 10 minutes of start time
**Tools:** Phone, SMS, CRM

### Steps
1. At 10 minutes past appointment start time, send a brief check-in text:
   > "Hi [Name], we have your appointment scheduled for right now! Just checking in — are you on your way? Let us know if you need to reschedule."
2. Log in CRM: "No-show — [date] — [service]"
3. Allow 15 more minutes. If no response, mark as no-show in scheduling system
4. Send the No-Show Follow-Up Email/Text (30 minutes after scheduled time):
   > "We missed you today! We'd love to get you rescheduled. Here's a link to book at your convenience: [booking link]"
5. Create re-engagement task: 1 week later — "No-show re-engagement outreach"

### AI Assist Opportunity
Use the "No-Show Re-Engagement Draft Prompt" if the customer hasn't responded after 1 week.

### Quality Checklist
- [ ] Check-in text sent at 10 minutes past
- [ ] CRM updated with no-show status
- [ ] Re-engagement message sent within 30 minutes
- [ ] Re-engagement task created for 1 week

### Escalation Rule
If the customer is a high-value account or a repeat no-show, escalate to the owner for a personal call.

## SOP R2-06: Lost Lead Reactivation

**Owner:** Front desk / Sales staff
**Trigger:** Lead has been in CRM for 30+ days with no conversion and no recent activity
**Tools:** CRM, Email, SMS

### Steps
1. Filter CRM for leads with last activity date > 30 days and status = "Open"
2. Review each lead — was there a reason they didn't move forward? Note in CRM if known
3. Send reactivation message using the Lost Lead Template:
   > "Hi [Name], it's been a little while since we connected. We'd love to check in — are you still thinking about [service]? We have some availability coming up and thought of you."
4. If no response within 3 days, send one additional follow-up
5. If no response after that, mark as "Inactive — No Response" in CRM (do not delete)
6. Schedule a 90-day recheck: "Quarterly re-engagement review"

### Quality Checklist
- [ ] 30-day inactive leads reviewed monthly
- [ ] Reactivation message personalized to service they inquired about
- [ ] Two-touch follow-up completed
- [ ] CRM status updated
- [ ] 90-day recheck scheduled

### Escalation Rule
If the lost lead was a high-value or referral lead, have the owner personally reach out.

## SOP R2-07: Review Request (Post-Service)

**Owner:** Front desk or post-service automation
**Trigger:** Service or project is completed
**Tools:** Email, SMS, Review platform (Google, Yelp, etc.)

### Steps
1. Within 24 hours of service completion, send the Review Request Template
2. Keep it brief and genuine:
   > "Thank you so much for trusting us with [service]! If you have a moment, we'd love if you'd leave us a Google review — it helps other customers find us. [link]"
3. Send via SMS first (highest open rate), email as backup if no click within 48 hours
4. Do not send more than 2 review requests to the same customer for the same service
5. Log in CRM: "Review request sent — [date]"
6. If customer leaves a review, thank them personally

### AI Assist Opportunity
Use the "Personalized Review Request Prompt" to generate a message that references the specific service the customer received.

### Quality Checklist
- [ ] Review request sent within 24 hours of completion
- [ ] Review platform link is current and functional
- [ ] CRM logged
- [ ] No duplicate requests sent

### Escalation Rule
If a customer recently had a complaint or expressed dissatisfaction — do not send a review request. Escalate to manager to confirm relationship is restored first.
