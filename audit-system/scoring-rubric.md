# OwnerOS AI — Readiness Score Rubric

Score the business across 10 dimensions based on intake form + discovery call findings.
Each dimension is scored 0–10. Total possible: 100 points.

---

## How to Score

1. Review the intake form responses
2. Use the discovery call to clarify any gaps
3. Score each dimension using the rubric below
4. Document your rationale for each score in the notes column
5. Total the scores for the OwnerOS Readiness Score

---

## Scoring Guide by Dimension

### Dimension 1: Lead Capture (0–10)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 9–10 | All leads from all channels land in one place automatically; source is tracked; no leads fall through |
| 7–8 | Most leads captured; 1–2 channels manual; some source tracking |
| 5–6 | Leads from 2–3 channels captured; some manual; occasional gaps |
| 3–4 | Multiple channels with inconsistent capture; frequent manual effort |
| 1–2 | Leads from different channels managed by different people; regular loss |
| 0 | No unified lead intake; largely phone + memory |

## Score Interpretation

| Range | Level | Meaning |
|-------|-------|---------|

| 21–40 | Basic systems, inconsistent execution | Some tools and processes exist but don't hold under pressure |
| 41–60 | Structure with gaps | Good foundation; missing several operational systems |
| 61–80 | Operational | Strong base; ready for optimization and expansion |
| 81–100 | High-performance operating system | Scalable, documented, delegated, and measured |
