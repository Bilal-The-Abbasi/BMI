## 5 Rooms Assessment

### Room 1: The Front Door — Score: 3/10

**Current state:**
- Leads arrive via website form, Instagram DM, phone call, and referrals
- Website form sends email notification to a shared inbox
- Instagram DMs checked once or twice per day
- Phone calls missed during busy treatment blocks with no callback system
- No source tracking — owner has no idea which channel produces the most clients

**Impact:**
- Estimated 5–8 leads per week never receive a response
- Average time to first response: 4–6 hours on good days
- Leads who don't hear back within 2 hours typically book with a competitor

**Quick wins available:**
- Set up missed call text-back automation (can be done in 1 day with GoHighLevel)
- Create a unified intake form that routes all web/social inquiries to one place
- Add a "Thank you for reaching out — we'll be in touch within the hour" auto-reply to website form

---

### Room 2: The Follow-Up Desk — Score: 2/10

**Current state:**
- When a lead is received, one staff member is "supposed to" respond
- No standard follow-up sequence exists
- After first contact, follow-up depends entirely on staff memory
- Quotes sent via email with no follow-up automation
- No reactivation process for past clients who haven't booked in 3+ months

**Impact:**
- Multiple prospects have been identified who never received follow-up after an initial inquiry
- One staff member described "a list of people I meant to call back" that hadn't been contacted in weeks
- No way to quantify how much revenue has been lost — but based on volume, it's significant

**Quick wins available:**
- Create a 3-step follow-up template (immediate response, day 2 check-in, day 7 "still interested?")
- Set up a weekly "no-response leads" review task for the front desk

---

### Room 3: The Playbook — Score: 2/10

**Current state:**
- 2 written SOPs found (appointment prep checklist, post-treatment instructions)
- All other processes exist in the owner's head or via verbal training
- Staff have asked the same FAQ questions 3+ times each (pricing, booking policies, what to say when something goes wrong)
- No brand voice guide — communication tone varies significantly by staff member

**Impact:**
- New staff take 6–8 weeks to feel confident — because there's nothing written to reference
- Owner spends approximately 5 hours/week answering staff questions that could be documented
- Customer experience varies significantly depending on who answers the phone

**Quick wins available:**
- Create a "Top 20 FAQ" document from the most common staff questions
- Establish a brand voice guide (2 pages: what we say, what we don't say, how we handle complaints)

---

### Room 4: The Workroom — Score: 3/10

**Current state:**
- Task assignment is primarily verbal or via Slack DM
- No central task board or project management tool
- Some recurring tasks (social content, monthly reports, supply ordering) have no assigned owner
- Owner is notified about problems primarily when they ask or when something is visibly wrong

**Impact:**
- Two marketing tasks have been sitting without owner review for 3 weeks
- Supply ordering has been delayed twice in the past month due to no assigned owner
- Owner spends significant time in "catch up" conversations to discover what's happening

**Quick wins available:**
- Set up a simple task board in ClickUp or Notion with weekly recurring checklists
- Assign owners to the top 10 recurring tasks

---

### Room 5: The Control Room — Score: 2/10

**Current state:**
- Owner reviews QuickBooks monthly for revenue
- Lead data exists in 3 different places and is not aggregated
- No weekly summary format — owner relies on staff check-ins and their own observation
- No AI-generated insights or pattern detection

**Impact:**
- Owner cannot answer these questions without 30+ minutes of manual pulling:
  - How many leads came in last week?
  - What's our average response time?
  - What's our booking conversion rate?
  - Which services are most requested?

**Quick wins available:**
- Create a simple weekly summary template the front desk fills out every Friday
- Set up a Google Data Studio (Looker Studio) dashboard connected to existing data sources

## Bottleneck Report

### Bottleneck 1: The Owner Is the Decision Point for Everything
**Evidence:** Every staff interview mentioned "I check with [owner's name] first." Staff do not have authority to resolve complaints, offer discounts, or approve schedule exceptions.
**Impact:** Owner handles 15–20 decisions per week that don't require their expertise.
**Recommendation:** Create decision rules in the Playbook — a simple escalation matrix so staff know what they can decide vs. what needs approval.

### Bottleneck 2: Lead Response Is Not Automated
**Evidence:** A test inquiry sent to the website form during the audit took 4 hours 22 minutes to receive a reply.
**Impact:** Competitors who respond in under 5 minutes are winning inquiries that Glow Med Spa would otherwise win.
**Recommendation:** GoHighLevel or similar CRM can send an automated SMS and email within 60 seconds of form submission.

### Bottleneck 3: No Written Process for Any Sales Conversation
**Evidence:** Staff describe pricing conversations as "I tell them what I know" — which varies by person.
**Impact:** Inconsistent pricing confidence, inconsistent service recommendations, inconsistent close rates.
**Recommendation:** Create a Sales Conversation SOP with a standard discovery question sequence, approved pricing language, and objection responses.
