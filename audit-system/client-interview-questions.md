# OwnerOS AI — Client Interview Questions (Discovery Call)

Use these during the 60-minute discovery session. You won't use all of them — pick based on what the intake form revealed. Prioritize the areas with the lowest apparent scores.

---

## Opening Questions (Start here)

1. "Walk me through a typical Monday. What's on your plate that shouldn't be?"
2. "When you think about the business running without you — what's the thing that worries you most?"
3. "What's the one operational thing that, if it were fixed, would change your week the most?"

---

## Lead and Follow-Up Questions

4. "Take me through what happens when a new lead comes in — step by step, from the moment they contact you."
5. "What happens to a lead if it comes in after hours, or when your front desk is with another customer?"
6. "How many follow-up touches does a prospect get before you give up?"
7. "Have you ever lost a deal and found out later it was because no one followed up? What happened?"
8. "What's your fastest response time to a new inquiry? What's your slowest?"
9. "If I sent an inquiry to your business right now, what would happen?"

---

## SOP and Process Questions

10. "If your front desk person quit tomorrow, what would the next person need to know to do the job?"
11. "What questions does your team ask you over and over that should be answered in a document somewhere?"
12. "What's the most common mistake your team makes that you've corrected more than twice?"
13. "If I asked three of your staff how to handle [common scenario], would they give me the same answer?"
14. "Have you ever tried to write SOPs? What happened?"

---

## Tool and Technology Questions

15. "Walk me through your tech stack — what tools does the business use every day?"
16. "When a lead comes in through your website, what happens to that data automatically?"
17. "Is there a tool you're paying for but not getting full value from?"
18. "How much time does your team spend copying information from one place to another in a typical week?"
19. "If I were to look at where your leads are, your tasks, and your customer communication — how many different tools would I need to look in?"

---

## Task and Team Questions

20. "At any given moment, how do you know what everyone on your team is working on?"
21. "What's the most common way something falls through the cracks — and how do you find out?"
22. "How do recurring tasks (weekly, monthly) get tracked and assigned?"
23. "Is there an approval queue somewhere — things that wait for you to sign off? How does that work today?"
24. "Do you have a regular team meeting? What happens there?"

---

## Owner and Bottleneck Questions

25. "On a scale of 1–10, how much does the business depend on you being personally available?"
26. "When was the last time you took a full week off without checking in? What happened?"
27. "What decisions can your team make without you right now? What can't they?"
28. "What task do you do repeatedly that you keep thinking you should hand off?"
29. "If the business were to grow 50% in the next year, where would it break first?"

---

## Reporting and Visibility Questions

30. "Where do you go right now to see how the business is performing?"
31. "What's a number or metric you look at most often? Where do you find it?"
32. "If I asked you how many leads came in last week, how long would it take you to answer that accurately?"
33. "Is there a weekly meeting where the business's performance is reviewed? What's discussed?"
34. "What would you want to see every Monday morning if you had a perfect dashboard?"

---

## AI and Future-State Questions

35. "Have you experimented with any AI tools — ChatGPT, Gemini, anything else? What did you try?"
36. "Are there any tasks in your business where you've thought 'this could probably be automated'?"
37. "What would you do with an extra 5 hours a week if operational tasks were handled automatically?"
38. "Is there anything in this conversation that's made you think 'we should really fix that'?"

---

## Closing Questions

39. "If you could design the ideal version of your business operations 12 months from now — what's different?"
40. "Is there anything about your business I should know that I haven't asked about?"
