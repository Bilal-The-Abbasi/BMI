# OwnerOS AI — Human Review Checklist

Use this checklist before any AI-generated content is approved for use in customer-facing or employee-facing contexts.

> **Policy:** No AI-generated content goes to a customer without human review and approval. This is non-negotiable.

---

## Human Review Checklist
**Content type:** [Email / SOP / Script / Dashboard Summary / Follow-up Sequence / Other]
**Client:** [Client Name]
**Reviewer:** [Reviewer Name]
**Date:** [Date]

---

## Part 1: Accuracy Check

- [ ] All factual statements in the content are accurate for this business
- [ ] Business name, location, contact info, and hours are correct
- [ ] Pricing mentioned (if any) is current and approved by the owner
- [ ] Service descriptions are accurate and not overstated
- [ ] Any statistics or claims are verified and sourced

---

## Part 2: Compliance Check

- [ ] Content does not make medical claims, medical recommendations, or clinical promises
- [ ] Content does not make legal claims, legal conclusions, or legal advice
- [ ] Content does not make guaranteed financial or revenue outcomes
- [ ] Content does not make insurance coverage claims without appropriate disclaimers
- [ ] Content complies with CAN-SPAM / CASL / TCPA rules (if applicable to this communication)
- [ ] Content is appropriate for the client's licensed activity (e.g., licensed contractor, licensed clinic, etc.)

*If any of the above are flagged — stop. Do not approve. Escalate to the client owner or their attorney.*

---

## Part 3: Brand Voice Check

- [ ] Tone matches the client's brand voice (professional, friendly, formal, casual — per brand guide)
- [ ] Language does not make promises the business cannot or does not intend to keep
- [ ] Language does not use competitor names in ways that could be problematic
- [ ] Content feels like it came from this business, not a generic template

---

## Part 4: Sensitivity Check

- [ ] Content does not contain information that should be private or confidential
- [ ] Content does not reference specific customers by name unless explicitly approved
- [ ] Content does not contain assumptions about customer health, finances, or personal situations
- [ ] Content does not contain language that could be perceived as discriminatory

---

## Part 5: Final Approval

- [ ] Content has been reviewed by at least one human from OwnerOS AI delivery team
- [ ] Content has been reviewed and approved by the client's designated contact
- [ ] Approved version is stored in the project folder with the date of approval
- [ ] Version sent to customer/staff is the approved version (not a draft)

## High-Risk Content Categories (Require Extra Review)

These content types require mandatory client approval before any use, even for internal testing:

| Content Type | Why Extra Review Is Required |
|-------------|------------------------------|
| Healthcare instructions or recommendations | HIPAA, medical liability |
| Legal rights statements | Legal advice, bar association rules |
| Insurance coverage statements | Insurance law, consumer protection |
| Pricing guarantees or "best price" claims | Consumer protection, accuracy |
| Testimonials or reviews (real or simulated) | FTC disclosure rules |
| Automated debt collection communications | FDCPA compliance |
| Messages to minors | COPPA, parental consent |
| Regulatory compliance claims ("HIPAA compliant," "licensed," etc.) | Must be verified by client |
