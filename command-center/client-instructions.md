# Welcome to Your OwnerOS Command Center

Your operating system is live. This guide explains how to use it.

## What This Is

The OwnerOS Command Center is your central hub — the one place where your business processes, tasks, prompts, and weekly reporting live.

You don't need to use every section every day. Here's what to focus on:

---

## Your Daily / Weekly Routine

### Every Morning (2–3 minutes)
→ Check **Room 1: The Front Door**
- Any new leads in the system?
- Any pending responses that need review?

### Every Week (5–10 minutes)
→ Review **Room 5: The Control Room**
- Open the Weekly Summary — completed by your front desk every Friday
- Review the key numbers: leads, bookings, follow-ups, open tasks

### As Needed
→ Refer to **Room 3: The Playbook**
- When staff have a question, the answer is here
- When you're onboarding someone new, start them here

## How to Use Each Room

### 🚪 Room 1: The Front Door
Shows what's happening with incoming leads. Check the CRM link in this room to see all active leads and their status.

### 📋 Room 2: The Follow-Up Desk
Your automated follow-up sequences run here. This room shows you what's active and lets you review outgoing messages before they send (for any requiring review).

### 📖 Room 3: The Playbook
Your SOP Library and FAQ Knowledge Base live here. When a staff member asks a question — point them here first.

**To add a new FAQ:**
1. Click "FAQ Knowledge Base"
2. Click "+ New"
3. Type the question, add the approved answer, set Category and Status

**To add a new SOP:**
1. Click "SOP Library"
2. Click "+ New"
3. Fill in the title, room, owner, trigger, and steps

### ⚙️ Room 4: The Workroom
Your task board. Staff log tasks here. You review the "Owner Approval" view when decisions need your input.

**To check what needs your approval:**
1. Open Room 4: The Workroom
2. Click the "Owner Approval Queue" view
3. Review and action each item

### 📊 Room 5: The Control Room
Your weekly visibility. The Weekly Summary is filled out by [designated staff] every Friday.

**To review the weekly summary:**
1. Open Room 5: The Control Room
2. Click "Weekly Summary Log"
3. Review the most recent entry (sorted by date, newest first)

## The AI Prompt Library

Your prompt library has ready-to-use AI prompts for common tasks: drafting follow-up emails, creating SOP drafts, summarizing call notes, responding to reviews, and more.

**To use a prompt:**
1. Open "AI Prompt Library"
2. Find the relevant prompt
3. Copy the Prompt Text
4. Paste into ChatGPT, Claude, or your preferred AI tool
5. Fill in the required Input (noted in each prompt)
6. **Review the output before using it** — all prompts include a review reminder
