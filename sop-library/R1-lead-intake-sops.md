# Room 1: The Front Door — Lead Intake SOPs

---

## SOP R1-01: New Lead Received — Web Form

**Owner:** Front Desk / Receptionist
**Trigger:** New form submission received from website
**Tools:** CRM (GHL/HubSpot), Email, Phone

### Steps
1. Check CRM notification or email for new lead submission (check every 30 minutes during business hours)
2. Open the lead record in CRM — confirm all fields populated (name, phone, email, inquiry type)
3. If auto-response has been sent, confirm it sent correctly (check automation log)
4. If no auto-response is configured, send the standard initial response template within 15 minutes
5. Categorize the lead: New Inquiry / Price Question / Existing Client / Referral / Other
6. Assign to the appropriate team member or queue based on lead type
7. Add a follow-up task in CRM or task board: "Follow-up call or text within 2 hours"
8. Log lead source in CRM source field (e.g., "Website — Contact Form")

### AI Assist Opportunity
Use the "Lead Summary Prompt" to draft a personalized first response based on the inquiry details.

### Quality Checklist
- [ ] Lead record created in CRM with correct fields
- [ ] Auto-response confirmed sent (or manual response sent within 15 min)
- [ ] Lead categorized and assigned
- [ ] Follow-up task created with due date
- [ ] Lead source logged

### Escalation Rule
Escalate to manager if: inquiry mentions a legal matter, complaint about a previous experience, or if the inquiry is from a media outlet or competitor.

- Initial Response Template (Room 2)
- Lead Intake CRM Fields Reference

## SOP R1-02: Missed Call Follow-Up

**Owner:** Front Desk / Receptionist
**Trigger:** Inbound call that went unanswered or was sent to voicemail
**Tools:** Phone system, CRM, SMS platform

### Steps
1. Review missed calls log at the top of every hour during business hours
2. Check CRM — did any of these numbers already have a lead record? If yes, update the record; if no, create one
3. Send a text message within 15 minutes using the Missed Call Text-Back Template:
   > "Hi, this is [Name] from [Business Name]. We missed your call! How can we help you today?"
4. If no response to text within 2 hours, attempt a callback
5. Log the outcome in CRM: "Missed call — text sent [time] — [response / no response]"
6. If no response within 24 hours, add to the follow-up sequence in CRM

### AI Assist Opportunity
If voicemail was left, use the "Voicemail Transcription Summary Prompt" to quickly understand the inquiry before calling back.

### Quality Checklist
- [ ] All missed calls reviewed
- [ ] Text sent within 15 minutes of missed call
- [ ] CRM record created or updated
- [ ] Callback attempted if no text response after 2 hours
- [ ] Outcome logged

### Escalation Rule
If a missed call leaves an urgent voicemail (emergency, complaint, health concern), escalate to manager immediately — do not wait for the standard timeline.

- Missed Call Text-Back Template
- Voicemail Response Script

## SOP R1-03: Website Form Response — Initial Contact

**Owner:** Front Desk / Receptionist
**Trigger:** New inquiry received via website contact form (general inquiry, not a specific booking form)
**Tools:** CRM, Email, Phone

### Steps
1. Review form submission — note the specific question or service they're asking about
2. Send personalized initial response within 30 minutes (business hours) using the Initial Response Template as a starting point
3. Do not use a generic "We'll be in touch" response — personalize to what they asked
4. If the inquiry is a pricing question, do not quote specific prices via email without owner guidance — use: "I'd love to give you accurate pricing — can we schedule a quick 5-minute call?"
5. Create CRM lead record with source: "Website — Contact Form"
6. Schedule follow-up task: if no response within 24 hours, send a brief check-in message
7. Log response sent in CRM activity feed

### AI Assist Opportunity
Use the "Website Inquiry Response Prompt" — provide the inquiry text and receive a draft personalized response for review.

### Quality Checklist
- [ ] Response sent within 30 minutes (business hours)
- [ ] Response is personalized (not generic)
- [ ] CRM record created
- [ ] Follow-up task scheduled
- [ ] No unverified pricing committed in writing without approval

### Escalation Rule
If inquiry mentions legal claims, safety concerns, or a previous negative experience — do not respond via standard template. Escalate to manager or owner before responding.

## SOP R1-04: New Lead from Referral

**Owner:** Front Desk / Receptionist
**Trigger:** A new lead contacts the business and mentions they were referred by a current or past client
**Tools:** CRM, Phone, Email

### Steps
1. Note the referral source name in the first interaction (ask directly: "Who referred you to us? I want to make sure we thank them.")
2. Create CRM lead record with source: "Referral — [Referrer Name]"
3. Send the Referral Thank-You Text/Email to the referrer the same day (see Referral Thank-You Template)
4. Assign the new lead to the owner or senior staff member (referrals get high-priority handling)
5. Schedule a discovery call or consultation within 24 hours
6. Log in CRM: referral source, referrer name, referrer's client status

### AI Assist Opportunity
Use the "Referral Thank-You Draft Prompt" to quickly generate a warm, personalized thank-you message.

### Quality Checklist
- [ ] Referrer name captured and logged in CRM
- [ ] Thank-you message sent to referrer same day
- [ ] New lead escalated to high priority
- [ ] Consultation scheduled within 24 hours

### Escalation Rule
If the referrer is a major account or strategic partner, notify the owner immediately.

## SOP R1-05: Inquiry from Social Media (DM / Comment)

**Owner:** Social media manager or designated front desk staff
**Trigger:** New message or comment on Instagram, Facebook, or other social platform expressing interest in services
**Tools:** Social media platform, CRM, Phone/Email

### Steps
1. Check all social media DMs and relevant comments at least twice per day (10 AM and 3 PM)
2. Respond to any inquiry within 2 hours during business hours
3. For DMs: respond with warmth + move toward a booking or consultation
   > "Hi [Name]! Thanks for reaching out. I'd love to tell you more — would you prefer I text/call you, or would you like to book a quick consult online? Here's our link: [booking link]"
4. Move the conversation off-platform as quickly as possible — get a phone number or email
5. Once contact info is obtained, create a CRM lead record with source: "Social — [Platform]"
6. If no response to your DM reply within 24 hours, send one follow-up message
7. Never post pricing in public comments — respond: "DM us for pricing!" or "Let me send you details"

### AI Assist Opportunity
Use the "Social Media Inquiry Response Prompt" for a warm, on-brand draft response.

### Quality Checklist
- [ ] DMs checked twice daily
- [ ] Response sent within 2 hours
- [ ] Contact info obtained and CRM record created
- [ ] Conversation moved off-platform
- [ ] No specific pricing posted in public comments

### Escalation Rule
Any negative public comment or complaint — do not respond with the standard template. Escalate to manager before responding. See SOP R2-09: Customer Complaint.
